## Coverage Notes

This directory covers VETERINARY medicine: the comparative, multi-species science of
animal health. It is deliberately distinct from `medicine/` (human clinical pharmacology
and practice), `disease/` (human disease catalog), and `pharmacology/` (drug mechanism
theory). Where a mechanism is identical across species, this directory cross-references
those rather than duplicating them, and focuses on what changes when the patient is a
cow, a parrot, a snake, or a cat.

Key connections: `zoology/` and `animal-phylogeny/` for the taxonomic and body-plan
substrate; `human-biology/` and `medicine/` for the comparative-physiology bridge;
`disease/` and `public-health/` for epidemiology, zoonoses, and surveillance;
`pharmacology/` for the receptor and metabolic mechanisms behind species drug differences.
The organizing idea is One Health — human, animal, and environmental health modeled as a
single coupled system.
