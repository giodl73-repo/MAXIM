# Gold Factory Wave

## Mission

Move from single scaled cohorts to a repeatable Gold Factory pattern:
agent-assisted scouting, noisy-candidate deferral, two independently proof-clean
12-guide cohorts, centralized writes, Da Vinci figure protection, registry
updates, and final validation.

## Agent Use

| Agent / Lane | Role | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `candidate-scout` explore agent | Read-only scout for non-Gold candidates with strong Gold shape | Surfaced useful clusters in AI engineering, agriculture, architecture, acoustics, aeronautics, and abstract algebra |
| Direct proof baseline | Mechanical triage of 48 attempted candidates | Rejected noisy math/aeronautics/agriculture files and retained two clean 12-guide cohorts |
| Coordinator write lane | Centralized cross-reference, invariant, registry, and evidence edits | Avoided background-agent write risk and kept records consistent |

## Baseline Findings

| Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|
| First broad candidate batch included many missing/noisy math and life-science paths, causing directory-level proof noise | Treated as scout signal only; no promotion claim made |
| Alternate 24-candidate batch exposed several clean AI, agriculture, architecture, and acoustics clusters | Split into two independently proof-clean 12-guide cohorts |
| Aeronautics 03-05 and several agriculture files had ASCII drift | Deferred from this factory wave rather than slowing the clean promotion lane |
| Cohorts lacked explicit cross-reference surfaces | Added Cross-References sections across all 24 promoted guides |
| Nested AI opening figures for evals/safety resolved to bridge diagrams under Da Vinci | Protected stable bridge figures for those two guides while retaining the opening diagrams in content |
