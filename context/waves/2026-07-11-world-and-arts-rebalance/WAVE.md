# World and Arts Rebalance

## Mission

Add missing historical and artistic practice while reducing the library's
Western-default compression.

## Scope

- `world-history/`: connected regional and global chronology with explicit
  African, Asian, Middle Eastern, American, Oceanian, and European lenses;
- `musicology/`: music history, ethnomusicology, performance institutions,
  recording, and listening traditions beyond Western formal theory;
- `studio-art/`: drawing, painting, sculpture, printmaking, installation, and
  conservation practice;
- targeted rebalance passes for art history, law, political science, medicine,
  psychology, and social science examples.

## Exit Gate

Coverage is not satisfied by one catch-all "non-Western" guide. Regional and
tradition choices must be explained, sourced, and reviewed for omission and
false equivalence.
