# Foundation and Taxonomy

## Mission

Establish the guide format, expand the first computing/data/language tracks, and
create the tracking machinery that let MAXIM see its own scope.

## Backfill Boundary

This wave is reconstructed after the fact from `HISTORY.md` and git log
subjects. It records coarse execution arcs and representative commits; it does
not pretend detailed pulse plans existed at the time.

## Close Evidence

This wave corresponds loosely to HISTORY phases 1-3: The Architect, The Scribe,
and The Taxonomist. The durable artifacts were the one-guide-per-topic format,
the ASCII/cheat-sheet style, the first broad field expansion, and `TRACKER.md`.
