## Operating Model

Each active wave keeps:

- `WAVE.md`: mission, scope, pulse table, gates, and carry-forwards.
- `pulses/NN+slug.md`: one executable slice with frontmatter, decisions,
  deliverables, and validation.
- `forks/pulse-NN.md`: materialized pulse context for agent execution.
- `panels/`: role reviews and consolidated findings.
- `CLOSE.md`: written when the wave is complete.

## Pulse Rules

- A pulse must be independently reviewable and testable.
- Every pulse names governing roles and validation commands.
- Quality claims must be explicit: mechanical proof, editorial review, expert
  adversarial review, or deferred carry-forward.
- Completed pulses keep their checkboxes checked; future pulses remain open.
- Wave status only advances after docs, proof gates, and review findings agree.

## History / Flair Bridge

- `HISTORY.md` remains the narrative source of truth for major sessions and card
  role claims.
- A wave closeout may recommend an `/honor` claim, but it must not claim a card
  automatically.
- Backfilled history should cite existing phases and commits rather than
  pretending a wave plan existed before the work.
