# Infrastructure and Complexity

## Mission

Complete the cross-domain systems layer needed to connect engineering,
operations, ecology, economics, and governance.

## Scope

- `civil-engineering/`: surveying, site/corridor design, codes, construction
  delivery, asset management, and integration across structural, geotechnical,
  transportation, water, and environmental engineering;
- `supply-chain-logistics/`: network design, inventory, warehousing, freight,
  procurement, resilience, and operations planning;
- `complexity-network-science/`: emergence, phase transitions, network
  topology, diffusion, cascades, adaptive systems, and model limits.

## Exit Gate

Each module imports rather than duplicates existing theory, demonstrates
cross-module reader tasks, and ships with authority bindings and expert review.
