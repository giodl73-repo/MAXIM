# MAXIM reuse boundary

MAXIM is a reference corpus, not a general-purpose code library or an
authoritative runtime knowledge service. Its proven portfolio reuse lane is
bounded reference grounding: a consumer names the concepts, mechanisms,
materials, comparisons, confusion points, or old-world/new-world bridges it
needs, while preserving MAXIM's evidence and certification status.

BANISH is the first recorded adopter. Its knowledge-system and panel-loop
gamepacks use MAXIM to identify research questions for game scenarios. They do
not copy MAXIM content into game rules or claim that thematic grounding proves
a simulation.

## Reusable contract

A downstream repository may reuse:

- stable guide paths and `md://` references to named MAXIM material;
- concept, mechanism, material, comparison, and confusion-point prompts;
- the landscape-first, layer-downward, decision-cheat-sheet authoring method;
- the documented expansion, review, Da Vinci invariant, and Gold-review
  methods; and
- validated MDPORT/CROP/FLETCH packs after a module has completed source-custody
  backfill and explicit publication.

Content reuse remains subject to MAXIM's CC BY-NC 4.0 license and attribution.
Commercial reuse requires separate written permission. Consumers must identify
the guide or future published pack they used rather than citing "MAXIM" as an
undifferentiated authority.

## Evidence limits

MAXIM's current public evidence tiers are part of the contract:

- only the guides listed in `context/gold/REGISTRY.md` under **Current Certified
  Gold** may be called Certified Gold;
- Candidate-Hardened guides retain useful proof and review provenance but are
  not individually certified;
- missing source custody must remain visible as `needs-source` or an equivalent
  blocked status; and
- no MDPORT pack or FLETCH registry exists merely because a guide or backfill
  ledger exists.

A consumer must not turn MAXIM prose into a legal, medical, scientific,
historical, safety, or simulation-validity claim without its own source and
domain review.

## Ownership boundary

MAXIM owns corpus text, guide structure, certification tiers, review records,
source-custody state, and publication admission.

Consumers own their product rules, scenario claims, transformations, excerpts,
attribution, and fitness review. BANISH owns playability, mechanics, simulation,
and gamepack status. MAXIM grounding may identify what BANISH should research;
it does not validate BANISH outcomes.

Downstream repositories should not vendor MAXIM source directories. The stable
distribution path is a committed module pack and registry after the flow in
`.mdloom/backfill/README.md` is complete.

## Adoption gate

A reuse claim requires a downstream manifest that records:

1. the named MAXIM guides, modules, prompts, or published packs consumed;
2. the certification and source-custody status;
3. the consumer-owned claim and transformation boundary;
4. attribution and license handling for copied content; and
5. a validation command or an explicit blocked-evidence status.

Until those fields exist, references to MAXIM are discovery leads, not admitted
evidence reuse.
