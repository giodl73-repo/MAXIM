# MAXIM fact-custody boundary

MAXIM guides are reference prose, not a standing guarantee that every number,
proper noun, date, formula, standard, version, or named historical detail has
completed a source-first fact-check wave. Broad synthesis can be useful and
still carry a load-bearing wrong specific.

This boundary closes `MAXIM-PF-02`: a confident specific inside an otherwise
strong guide must stay visibly provisional until it has guide-local evidence,
source-custody status, and role review.

## Specific-fact rule

A MAXIM surface must not be reused as evidence for a specific fact unless the
consumer can name:

1. the guide path and exact claim being reused;
2. whether the guide is Certified Gold, Candidate-Hardened, source-custody,
   needs-source, or unresolved;
3. the fact-check wave, source-custody record, or audit row supporting that
   number, name, date, formula, standard, version, or historical detail;
4. the Reference Integrity Auditor review outcome for the changed or reused
   claim; and
5. the consumer-owned verification needed for legal, medical, scientific,
   historical, safety, compliance, product, or simulation claims.

If any field is missing, the guide is a research lead or orientation surface,
not fact authority.

## MAXIM owner responsibility

MAXIM maintainers should repair wrong specifics source-first:

- edit the numbered source guide, not generated source-corpus artifacts;
- cite the correction in an audit, source-custody record, or review note;
- regenerate derived artifacts when the touched module participates in the
  source-corpus pipeline; and
- keep candidate, unresolved, and `needs-source` states visible instead of
  promoting the guide because its surrounding synthesis is strong.

## Consumer responsibility

Downstream consumers must not cite "MAXIM" as an undifferentiated authority for
specific facts. They must carry the named guide, claim boundary, custody status,
and validation or blocked-evidence status into their own adoption manifest.

This is stricter than the general reuse boundary because a single bad number,
name, date, formula, standard, or version can invalidate the downstream claim
even when the guide remains useful as a reading path.
