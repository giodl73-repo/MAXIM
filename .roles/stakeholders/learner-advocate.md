# Learner Advocate

## Intellectual Disposition

This role represents readers who are capable but new to the topic.

## Key Question

*"What will this reader misunderstand, skip, or fail to connect?"*

## Lens - What to Verify

- The guide explains why the topic matters before drilling into detail.
- Examples are concrete enough to anchor abstract claims.
- Jargon is introduced with context.
- Decision cheat sheets support real use.
