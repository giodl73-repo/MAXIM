## Coverage Notes

Marketing is the discipline of creating, communicating, and delivering value to a
defined set of customers profitably. This directory covers the field from strategy
down to measurement: the marketing concept and the mix (4 Ps / 7 Ps); how customers
actually decide (consumer behavior, bridging to `behavioral-economics/`); how firms
choose whom to serve and how to be perceived (segmentation, targeting, positioning);
how brands accumulate and are valued as equity (Keller CBBE, Aaker); the two mix
elements with the deepest quantitative spine — product/price (life cycle, elasticity,
value-based pricing, drawing on `economics/`) and promotion (IMC, media math); how
goods reach buyers (channels and omnichannel); how marketers learn what is true
(research methods, conjoint, A/B testing, drawing on `statistics-applied/`); the
modern digital surface (search, social, programmatic, attribution); and the unit
economics that decide whether any of it pays (CAC, LTV, ROAS, MMM vs MTA).

Cross-references: `economics/` (demand, elasticity, market structure), `behavioral-economics/`
(decision biases, framing, nudges), `organizational-behavior/` (strategy, change),
`media-studies/` (media effects, attention), `statistics-applied/` (sampling, inference,
experiment design). Marketing does not re-derive economic theory or general strategy —
it imports them and adds the customer-facing discipline on top.
