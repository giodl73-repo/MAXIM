## Coverage Notes

Naval architecture and marine engineering: the design of ships and marine
vehicles. Covers hydrostatics and intact/damage stability (Archimedes,
metacentric height GM, righting arm GZ), hull form and the resistance
decomposition (frictional, wave-making, form) under Froude scaling, propulsion
(propeller theory, thrust, cavitation, prime movers), the ship as a hull girder
(hogging/sagging, longitudinal strength), seakeeping (motions in waves, RAOs,
slamming, stabilization), onboard marine systems, shipbuilding and classification
societies, the taxonomy of ship types, and offshore/marine engineering.

Distinct from `fluid-dynamics/` (which owns Navier-Stokes, boundary layers, and
general free-surface theory — this module applies those results to floating
bodies) and from `structural/` (which owns beam theory and material mechanics —
this module applies them to the hull girder). Cross-references `mechanical/`
(prime movers, shafting), `transportation/` (ships in the global logistics
network), and `materials/` (steel, aluminium, composites for marine service).
